COMMERCIAL PROPERTY AERIAL FOOTAGE

What We Capture

Clean establishing shots and full-site overviews of commercial buildings and grounds — the building itself, the surrounding property, parking areas, and approach. Shot to present the property well, not to pick it apart.

Use Cases

Commercial aerial footage works well for marketing materials, website and listing photography, and general site documentation for property managers and owners. It's a fast way to get a current, professional-looking overview of a property without renting a lift or scheduling a helicopter.

Delivery

Footage is delivered the same day — on-site via USB, sent remotely, or uploaded as an unlisted YouTube link. All files are raw 4K 60fps as captured. A complimentary backup copy is retained for 90 days.

Looking for Roof or Condition Documentation Instead?

If you need footage of standing water, drainage, or exterior wear on a building — for your own records or to track how a property is holding up over time — see our Property Condition page instead.
