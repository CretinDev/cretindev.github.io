AERIAL DOCUMENTATION FOR INSTITUTIONS

What Makes This Footage Useful as Evidence

The footage we deliver is raw, unedited, and timestamped at the camera level at the time of capture. There is no post-processing, colour grading, or editing applied. What you receive is a direct record of what the drone's camera saw, when it saw it. That chain of custody — from capture to delivery without modification — is what makes aerial footage useful in legal, insurance, and regulatory contexts.

We use a DJI Mini 4 Pro, a sub-250g aircraft that operates quietly and at low altitude. This classification means we can document sites discreetly, without the physical presence or visibility of a larger commercial drone. Footage is delivered the same day via USB on-site, secure file transfer, or unlisted private link.

Insurance Adjusters

Pre-existing condition documentation is one of the most practically useful applications of aerial footage in an insurance context. A timestamped aerial survey of a property before a weather event, construction project, or change of occupancy gives an adjuster a clear before-state that can be compared against post-incident footage or a physical inspection. The question of whether damage is pre-existing or newly caused is significantly easier to resolve when both states are documented from the same aerial perspective.

Property Disputes and Legal Teams

Aerial documentation of property condition at a specific point in time provides an objective visual record for use in property litigation, contractor liability disputes, boundary disagreements, and neighbour damage claims. Ground-level photographs show a limited angle. Aerial footage shows the full context — property boundaries, surrounding conditions, adjacent properties, and site layout — all from a perspective that is difficult to dispute or misrepresent.

Mortgage Lenders and Underwriting

Lenders holding mortgages on residential or commercial properties benefit from a documented aerial record of collateral condition. At the time of a transaction, a foreclosure, or a refinance, aerial footage of the property provides a current condition assessment that supplements the formal appraisal process. For lenders managing multiple properties, periodic aerial documentation builds a portfolio-level condition record that is useful for risk management and due diligence.

Regulatory and Environmental Compliance

Aerial documentation has been used directly in regulatory enforcement actions in this region. In one case, unauthorized drainage of a protected wetland by a construction company adjacent to residential properties was documented aerially at the request of affected homeowners. That footage was submitted anonymously to the North Bay-Mattawa Conservation Authority. The NBMCA investigated, determined the construction company had no legal authorization for the drainage activity, and initiated enforcement proceedings.

For the Ministry of Natural Resources, conservation authorities, municipal bylaw enforcement, and environmental compliance officers, aerial documentation of site conditions before, during, and after an incident provides a factual record that is difficult to contest.

Discretion and Delivery

Engagements for legal and regulatory clients are handled with full discretion. We do not disclose client identities, site locations, or footage contents to third parties. Delivery is arranged directly — on-site USB transfer, encrypted remote transfer, or private link. Footage is retained on our end for 90 days as a complimentary backup and then deleted unless otherwise arranged. If your engagement requires specific handling procedures, contact us directly to discuss.
