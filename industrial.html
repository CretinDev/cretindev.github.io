SITE PROGRESSION & AERIAL RECORDS

Site Progression Records

Industrial and construction sites change constantly. An aerial survey at each major project phase produces a timestamped visual record of site development that no ground-level photography can replicate. From above, the full scope of a site — equipment placement, structure progress, earthwork, perimeter changes — is visible in a single frame.

These records serve multiple purposes simultaneously: they document what was done and when, they provide a reference point if a dispute arises about project scope or timeline, and they give project leadership a clear view of site status without requiring a physical visit.

Stakeholder and Project Management Reporting

Aerial footage is the most efficient way to communicate site progress to people who aren't on the ground. A five-minute aerial survey can produce a complete visual update for ownership groups, investors, or clients without pulling anyone away from their own work. Footage is delivered the same day and can be shared immediately as an unlisted link.

For projects with recurring reporting requirements — weekly, monthly, or milestone-based — we can schedule regular shoots that build a consistent aerial archive over the life of the project.

Compliance and Site Integrity

Aerial documentation provides an objective record of site conditions at a given point in time. If questions arise about site boundaries, unauthorized changes to site layout, environmental compliance, or the condition of adjacent properties before and after work begins, the aerial record provides a reference that isn't dependent on anyone's recollection.

Pre-construction aerial surveys of a site and its surrounding area create a baseline that protects contractors from liability for conditions that existed before they arrived.

Delivery

All footage is delivered raw, unedited, 4K at 60fps. Delivery on-site via USB, remote file transfer, or unlisted YouTube link the same day. A complimentary backup copy is retained for 90 days. Recurring contract rates are available for projects requiring regular documentation over an extended timeline.
