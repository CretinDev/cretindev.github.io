ROOF & EXTERIOR CONDITION FROM ABOVE

What We Document

Standing water, blocked drains, membrane wear, and other exterior condition details that are difficult or impossible to see from the ground. A drone pass after rainfall or on a regular schedule gives you an accurate, current picture of what a roof or exterior surface actually looks like.

Not a Substitute for a Professional Inspection

We are not licensed building inspectors or engineers, and this footage is not a certified inspection or engineering report. It's visual documentation — a clear record of what a property looked like on a given day. If you need a formal assessment or certified report, use this footage alongside a qualified inspector, not in place of one.

How It's Used

Property owners and managers use this footage to keep a visual record over time, to compare before-and-after condition following a storm or repair, or simply to have current documentation on hand. The footage is yours to use, share, or pass along however you need — to a contractor, an inspector, an insurer, or just for your own records.

Delivery

Footage is delivered the same day — on-site via USB, sent remotely, or uploaded as an unlisted YouTube link. All files are raw 4K 60fps as captured. A complimentary backup copy is retained for 90 days.

Want a current record of a roof or exterior? Same day delivery standard.
