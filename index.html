HOW WE OPERATE

01

Book

Send your property address and preferred timing. We'll verify airspace, confirm weather windows, and schedule the flight.

02

Fly

We arrive on-site, perform a complete aerial survey with the DJI Mini 4 Pro, and capture full documentation of the property.

03

Receive

Raw footage delivered on-site via USB, sent remotely, or uploaded as an unlisted YouTube link. No editing, no delay. A secondary copy is retained as a complimentary backup for 90 days.

Footage Raw, Unedited, 4K 60fps
All files are delivered exactly as captured — no colour grading, compression, or editing applied. 4K resolution at 60 frames per second, straight from the drone's camera.
Delivery USB, remote transfer, or YouTube link
Files can be transferred to a USB drive on-site, sent remotely via file transfer within 24 hours, or uploaded as an unlisted YouTube link — just click and watch, no downloads required. A secondary copy is retained for 90 days as a complimentary backup.
Classification Sub-250g Micro Drone
Aircraft weighing under 250g fall into the micro drone category under Transport Canada RPAS regulations. This classification reduces airspace restrictions and simplifies compliance for most survey operations.
